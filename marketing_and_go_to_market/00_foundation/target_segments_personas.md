# Target Segments & Personas (Solana-native)

We acquire in a deliberate order: a small, high-status **beachhead** that confers credibility, then expand to the speculative majority who follow status and points. Each persona enters a *specific* growth loop — we never market generically.

## Segment priority & sequencing

1. **Beachhead (Phase 0–1):** Sharps + Skill-gamers + a tight set of KOL-Operators. They set the culture and produce the first legendary hands.
2. **Amplifiers (Phase 1):** KOLs/creators and Whales — distribution and high-stakes spectacle.
3. **Majority (Phase 2+):** Degens/speculators pulled by points/token gravity and clips.
4. **Ambient (ongoing):** Spectators/railbirds who may never deposit big but are a distribution and (future) spectator-market layer.

---

## Persona 1 — The Sharp (Trader-Operator) ★ beachhead
- **Who:** Solana perps/DeFi trader (Drift, Jupiter, marginfi, Kamino). Believes they can read the market and out-trade peers. Lives on CT.
- **Where they are:** trading-focused CT, perps Discords, trading-comp leaderboards.
- **Hook:** "Stop trading against the market — *compete against other traders* for a pot and a rank." Skill + status + provable fairness.
- **Loop they enter:** Status (climb rating → The Council) + Clip (post their sharp reads).
- **Risk:** they'll smell overclaiming instantly → lead with honest skill framing + on-chain fairness.

## Persona 2 — The Skill-Gamer (Poker/GTO crowd) ★ beachhead
- **Who:** online-poker players, GTO/strategy nerds, ex-PokerStars, chess/strategy gamers now in crypto.
- **Where:** poker Twitter, strategy Discords, Solana gaming circles.
- **Hook:** "No-limit poker where your hand is a hidden conviction and the board is a live market." Side pots, reads, fold equity — the real thing, on-chain.
- **Loop:** Status + Chamber (team competition) — they bring rigor and teach the meta (content).
- **Risk:** they demand mechanical depth/integrity → showcase the rules canon, replays, fairness.

## Persona 3 — The Degen / Speculator (majority)
- **Who:** memecoin + airdrop-farming Solana retail. Driven by points, speculation, and FOMO.
- **Where:** mainstream CT, Telegram alpha groups, points-meta dashboards.
- **Hook:** Season 0 points + a credible token path + visible status to chase. "Early Operators are pricing in."
- **Loop:** Speculation/points → Referral (Houses) → Clip. They are the volume layer; they make markets and noise.
- **Risk:** mercenary churn → design points to reward *play and retention*, not just deposits; anti-sybil.

## Persona 4 — The KOL / Creator (amplifier)
- **Who:** CT personalities, streamers, trading educators, Solana-native influencers.
- **Where:** their own audiences — the highest-leverage distribution on the chain.
- **Hook:** become a founding **House** owner + Council fast-lane + revenue/points share. Their *status compounds when their audience plays.*
- **Loop:** KOL-as-Operator → House referral loop (their audience fills their House) + Clip (they create the legendary hands). See `01_acquisition/kol_and_creator_program.md`.
- **Risk:** pay-for-post mercenaries → align on ownership/upside, not flat fees, so incentives are recruiting + retention.

## Persona 5 — The Whale / High-Roller (amplifier)
- **Who:** high-net-worth crypto, status-seeking, wants the top table and to be *seen* there.
- **Where:** private Telegram, OTC circles, high-tier KOL orbits.
- **Hook:** the premium frame itself — the elite table, Grand Operator aspiration, exclusive Houses, concierge.
- **Loop:** Status (Grand Operator chase) + spectacle (their big hands are marquee clips).
- **Risk:** pay-to-win optics could poison the skill brand → status remains skill/volume-earned, not deposit-bought; whales get *stakes*, not *rank shortcuts*.

## Persona 6 — The Spectator / Railbird (ambient)
- **Who:** crypto-curious, watchers, fans of the drama; may never play big.
- **Where:** anywhere a clip lands; the live-games dashboard.
- **Hook:** the reveal drama, rivalries, Council storylines; (Phase 2) the spectator prediction market = skin in the game without a seat.
- **Loop:** Clip consumption → light play / sharing → (future) spectator betting.
- **Risk:** never converts → that's fine; their *attention and shares* are the product. Optimize watch + share, not just conversion.

## Persona → loop → first-touch surface (cheat sheet)

| Persona | Primary loop | First touch |
|---------|--------------|-------------|
| Sharp | Status + Clip | trading CT, perps partners, trading-comp crossovers |
| Skill-gamer | Status + Chamber | poker/strategy CT, mechanics deep-dives |
| Degen | Speculation/points + Referral | points meta, Blinks, alpha groups |

**Rule:** every campaign names its persona and the loop it ignites. A campaign that doesn't start a loop doesn't ship.

# Lexicon & Status System — the growth engine in disguise

The community hierarchy is **the single most important growth asset** Syndicate has. It is a status civilization, and the chase for status does our acquisition, retention, and virality simultaneously. Treat every term as a proper noun and a load-bearing mechanic — not flavor.

## The lexicon (canonical, use everywhere)

| Layer | Name | What it is |
|-------|------|------------|
| Platform | **Syndicate** | the institution / the arena |
| Member | **Operator** | every player; the identity you wear |
| Guild / team | **Chamber** | a competitive team Operators join; collective standing |
| Private group | **House** | an invite-only room/crew an Operator founds or joins |
| Top 100 players | **The Council** | the public elite; ranked, visible, revered |
| Highest rank | **Grand Operator** | the apex; one (or few) at the summit; living lore |

## Why a status ladder is the right engine on Solana

Solana retail is driven by **status + speculation**. People don't just want returns — they want to be *seen* winning. A visible, on-chain, hard-to-fake rank ladder converts that drive into our growth loops:

- **Acquisition:** rank is a flex; flexing is free advertising. The Council leaderboard is a recruiting poster.
- **Retention:** you don't churn out of a game where you have *rank to defend* and a *Chamber depending on you*.
- **Virality:** status is only legible relative to others → you recruit rivals and teammates to make your standing mean something (and Houses literally need members).
- **Defensibility:** reputation is non-portable. A whale can buy chips anywhere; they cannot buy a Council seat or Chamber legend. That's the moat.

## The ladder, tier by tier (and its growth job)

### Operator (the identity)
- Earned the moment you take a real seat. Every Operator has a **public profile**: rank, season stats, badges, Chamber, House, lifetime hands, signature reveals.
- On-chain reputation: wallet-bound, provable, screenshot-native.
- **Growth job:** the unit of identity people attach ego to. Make the profile a *luxury object* people want to share.

### Chamber (the team sport)
- Operators join a **Chamber** (a guild). Chambers have collective leaderboards, season standings, shared rituals, and Chamber-vs-Chamber events.
- **Growth job — retention + recruiting:** belonging multiplies retention (you let teammates down by churning); Chambers compete to recruit strong Operators → members evangelize. Chambers are the ambassador program in disguise (`04_community/ambassador_and_chamber_program.md`).

### House (the invite loop)
- A **House** is an invite-only crew/room an Operator founds. Houses get their own private tables, internal leaderboards, optional **rake/points share to the founder**, and a House page.
- **Growth job — virality (K-factor):** founding a House gives you a *direct, selfish reason to recruit* (your House needs Operators to fill tables; founders share in House activity). This is the referral engine made social and status-bearing rather than a coupon. (`03_virality_network_effects/chambers_and_houses.md`)

### The Council (the aspiration)
- The **top 100 Operators**, publicly ranked, refreshed each season. Council members get visible insignia, priority access, governance voice, event invites, and revenue/points multipliers.
- **Growth job — aspiration + content:** a scarce, visible summit creates a permanent climb. Council movements ("X entered The Council," "Y was dethroned") are recurring CT content. Scarcity (100) makes it real status, not a participation trophy.

### Grand Operator (the lore)
- The **apex** — the #1 (or a tiny ruling tier), crowned each season with ceremony. The Grand Operator is a *character* in the brand: featured, interviewed, memorialized in a hall of champions.
- **Growth job — mythology:** every status civilization needs a king. The Grand Operator coronation is a marquee, broadcastable event (`04_community/rituals_events_and_seasons.md`) and an aspirational north star for every Operator.

## Status must be *earned*, *visible*, and *non-buyable*

Three rules that keep the ladder valuable (a status system you can buy is worthless):

1. **Earned by play + recruiting**, never by deposit size alone. Rank reflects skill-over-volume (poker-style rating) and contribution (founding Houses, growing Chambers).
2. **Visible everywhere** — profiles, leaderboards, Blinks, the app, and shareable cards. If it's invisible, it can't drive flexing.
3. **Anti-sybil & anti-pay-to-win** — proof-of-personhood and skill-weighted rating prevent farming the ladder; protects the prestige that makes it worth chasing (`05_economy_incentives/incentive_and_anti_sybil_design.md`).

## Progression spine (how an Operator climbs)

```
First seat (Operator)
   → join/found a House (invite loop fires)
   → join a Chamber (team retention)
   → climb season rating (skill + volume)
   → enter The Council (top 100; public elite)
   → crowned Grand Operator (apex; lore)
```

Each arrow is a designed growth event with its own trigger, reward, and shareable artifact (detailed in `02_activation_retention/progression_and_ranks.md`).

## Terminology guardrails

- Always capitalize: Operator(s), Chamber(s), House(s), The Council, Grand Operator.
- Never substitute generic words ("users," "teams," "VIPs," "leaderboard top") in any external surface — the lexicon is in-group signaling and brand equity.
- New surfaces (token, NFT access, events, merch) must extend the lexicon, not invent parallel terms.

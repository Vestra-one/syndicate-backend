# Growth Risk Register & Mitigations

The flywheel (`../00_foundation/growth_model_and_flywheel.md`) is powerful precisely because it compounds — which means a mismanaged loop compounds *against* us just as fast. This register catalogues the growth risks that could break the compounding, rated by severity and likelihood, with mitigations tied to our actual mechanics and the canon (ANTI-CASINO; Royal × Institutional × Elite × Premium; status civilization; the seven loops; the phase sequence in `phased_gtm_roadmap.md`).

The thread running through every mitigation: **our defenses are the same mechanics that drive growth.** Points earned by *playing and retaining* defeat farmers and also channel mercenary energy. Earned, non-buyable status defeats pay-to-win optics and also *is* the retention engine. We don't bolt on safety — the design is the safety.

**Severity** = damage if it occurs (Critical / High / Med / Low). **Likelihood** = probability over Phases 0–3 (High / Med / Low).

## Cross-cutting principle

Read the register top to bottom and a pattern emerges: **the highest-severity risks (3, 4, 5) are all brand/compliance/cold-start, and all three are mitigated by the same two assets — practice mode and the ANTI-CASINO skill frame.** Practice mode is simultaneously the ungeofenced funnel (defeats #3), the proof it's skill not gambling (defeats #4), and the zero-risk liquidity seed (defeats #5). That is not a coincidence — it is why practice mode is open and load-bearing in the flywheel, not an afterthought.

Likewise the highest-*likelihood* risks (1, 2, 4, 5, 9) are all defused by mechanics that *also* drive growth: points-as-retained-play, skill-weighted non-buyable status, manufactured-but-legitimate drama, and Houses concentrating liquidity. **We do not trade growth for safety; the same design buys both.**

## One line

Every mitigation here is also a growth mechanic — practice mode, earned non-buyable status, points-as-retained-play, manufactured drama, Houses-as-liquidity. The discipline is to **sequence** (token only at scale, virality only after retention, crossovers only when the brand can carry them, per `phased_gtm_roadmap.md`) and to treat the premium ANTI-CASINO frame as a hard rule. Defend the brand and the flywheel defends itself.

# Phased GTM Roadmap — Igniting the Loops in Sequence

This is the execution arc for the master flywheel (`../00_foundation/growth_model_and_flywheel.md`). The single non-negotiable discipline: **do not light all seven loops at once.** We optimize in the canonical order — **PMF → Retention → Virality → Community → Ecosystem Dominance** — because each stage is fuel for the next. Virality before retention burns the brand. Token/speculation before retention lands on vaporware. So the loops ignite in a deliberate sequence, one phase building the substrate the next phase exploits.

The seven loops (from the flywheel): (1) Clip/content · (2) House referral / K-factor · (3) Status/Council · (4) Chamber/team · (5) Speculation/points · (6) KOL-as-Operator · (7) Ecosystem/partners.

The north star is constant across every phase: **Weekly Active Operators who reach a contested reveal**, plus **K-factor** and **W4 retention** (`../07_growth_ops_metrics/north_star_and_kpis.md`). Phases differ in *which loop we are proving*, not in the number we watch.

Timeline is approximate and gated by exit criteria, not the calendar — **a phase ends when its criteria are met, never because time elapsed.**

## Phase 0 — Stealth & Genesis Council (≈ Months 0–3)

**Primary goal.** Prove the reveal sells and the culture exists *before* anyone can buy in. Establish PMF on the core mechanic: does a contested SEALED-REVEAL produce a moment people *want* to share? Build the Genesis Council waitlist as a scarce, status-bearing artifact.

**Target audience / persona.** A tiny, hand-picked cohort: the Sharp Trader and Status Climber (`../00_foundation/target_segments_personas.md`), plus anchor Solana KOLs recruited as Founding Operators. Quality over quantity — these faces *define* the brand.

**Loops to IGNITE (sequence).**
- **(1) Clip/content** — first. Manufacture beautiful reveal moments and prove they circulate.
- **(6) KOL-as-Operator** — in parallel. Anchor KOLs get a House + Council fast-lane + upside so their status compounds when they play.
- *Deliberately dark:* House (2), Chamber (4), Status/Council at scale (3), and absolutely **no points/token (5)** and no broad ecosystem push (7). Retention isn't proven; virality and speculation would burn the brand.

**Channels.** Private CT seeding, invite-only Discord, hand-built exhibition matches, co-streams with anchor KOLs. Close the P0 partnerships (Switchboard, Phantom, Dialect, Helius — `../06_ecosystem_distribution/partnerships_pipeline.md`).

**Headline tactics.**
- **Genesis Council waitlist** — invite-only, ranked, scarce. The waitlist itself is a status object; entry is broadcastable.
- **Founding Operator program** — recruit anchor KOLs as Operators with Houses and a Council fast-lane.
- **First reveal clips** from the Clip Engine (`../03_virality_network_effects/spectator_and_clip_engine.md`), seeded into CT to test shareability.
- **Practice mode live and open** (ungeofenced) so the aha moment is frictionless (`../02_activation_retention/onboarding_and_aha_moment.md`).

**KPIs / exit criteria.**
- Reveal-clip share rate clears a threshold (people share unprompted).
- Activation: first-SEALED-REVEAL completion rate in practice mode is strong.
- Early W4 retention signal positive among the seed cohort.
- Genesis Council waitlist filled with credible, on-brand names.
- **Exit when:** PMF is evidenced (the reveal demonstrably sells) and retention shows a positive W4 signal — *not* before.

**Ops / headcount.** Lean founding pod: founder-led BD for P0 partners, 1 community/CT lead, 1 Clip Engine + product engineer, design for the reveal-card aesthetic. Voice is owned in-house from day one — never outsourced.

## Phase 1 — Invite-Only Launch (≈ Months 3–7)

**Primary goal.** Turn retained Operators into **recruiters and teams.** Prove the K-factor: do Operators found Houses and fill tables because it's in their self-interest? Solidify retention with ranks to defend and Chamber obligation.

**Target audience / persona.** Expanding waitlist waves — the broader Sharp Trader / Status Climber base, recruited *by* existing Operators. The audience now arrives via Houses and Council aspiration, not hand-picking.

**Loops to IGNITE (sequence).**
- **(2) House referral / K-factor** — the headline loop of this phase. Houses go live; founding one gives a selfish, social reason to recruit.
- **(3) Status/Council** — The Council goes public; movements become recurring CT drama.
- **(4) Chamber/team** — Chambers launch; team standing converts solo churn into social stickiness.
- *Still dark:* points/token (5) stays off — retention is being proven, not yet bankable; broad ecosystem (7) is seeded but not at full power.

**Channels.** CT (now with public Council drama), Blinks/Actions as the K-factor rail (`../06_ecosystem_distribution/solana_distribution_playbook.md`), trading Discords/TG as House+Chamber homes, broadening KOL program. Activate P1 partners (Pyth, Backpack, Drift, quest platforms — lightly).

**Headline tactics.**
- **Houses live with "Join my House" Blinks** — every founder gets a one-tap on-chain recruiting rail.
- **The Council goes public** — leaderboard, insignia, "X entered / Y dethroned" as engineered drama.
- **First Chamber-vs-Chamber events** — team rivalry seeds recruiting and retention.
- **Invite waves** create artificial scarcity; each wave is a CT moment.
- **First Grand Operator coronation** as a broadcast tentpole (`../04_community/rituals_events_and_seasons.md`).

**KPIs / exit criteria.**
- **K-factor approaching or above 1** (invites sent × conversion per invite — the growth equation).
- W4 retention holds as the cohort scales (retention doesn't collapse with volume).
- Median Operators-per-House and active-Chamber count climbing.
- Weekly Active Operators reaching a contested reveal growing without paid acceleration.
- **Exit when:** K-factor is durably ≥ ~1 *and* W4 retention holds at scale — the loops self-sustain without hand-holding.

**Ops / headcount.** Add: community ops (House/Chamber support, Discord moderation), a BD lead for P1 partners, a second content/clip producer, anti-sybil/trust engineering ahead of the points program. Begin compliance groundwork on geofencing.

## Phase 2 — Scale (≈ Months 7–15)

**Primary goal.** Make the loops self-sustaining at volume and **turn on speculation + ecosystem** at full power — now that they land on proven retention, not vaporware. Scale Weekly Active Operators an order of magnitude while CAC falls.

**Target audience / persona.** The broader Solana base — including the **airdrop farmer / mercenary capital** cohort, now *productively channeled* (points earned by playing and retaining), plus the spectators converted by viral clips.

**Loops to IGNITE (sequence).**
- **(5) Speculation/points** — the headline addition. Season 0 points + token path go live; mercenary energy converts to retained play and free shilling (by design, `../05_economy_incentives/`).
- **(7) Ecosystem/partners** at full power — wallet placements, quests, DeFi cross-promos, points aggregators.
- All prior loops (1–4, 6) now running and compounding together.

**Channels.** Full distribution stack from `../06_ecosystem_distribution/solana_distribution_playbook.md`: wallet placement (Phantom/Backpack), quest platforms, points aggregators, oracle/perp co-marketing, co-streams at tentpole scale, CT always-on. Activate P2 partners (Jupiter, marginfi, Kamino, Zeta, points aggregators).

**Headline tactics.**
- **Season 0 points program** with a transparent, never-overclaimed token narrative; anti-sybil gated from day one.
- **Public / open-access launch** (real-money tiers **geofenced**; practice open) — the invite scarcity converts to broad access.
- **Quest campaigns** mapped to activation milestones across partner platforms.
- **Wallet featured placements** and **DeFi cross-quests** route the native persona in via Blinks.
- **Recurring seasons** with Grand Operator coronations as the cultural metronome.

**KPIs / exit criteria.**
- Weekly Active Operators reaching a contested reveal up ~10× vs. Phase 1 exit.
- W4 retention holds *through* the speculative cohort (points farmers retain, not bounce).
- Paid/partner share of new Operators falling as a fraction of growth (loops carry it).
- Sybil/farm rate below threshold (anti-sybil holding — `../05_economy_incentives/incentive_and_anti_sybil_design.md`).
- **Exit when:** growth is loop-driven (paid trending toward a minority of new Operators), retention survives the mercenary influx, and ecosystem rails are live and producing.

**Ops / headcount.** Scale: dedicated partnerships team, growth/analytics function owning the metric tree (`../07_growth_ops_metrics/north_star_and_kpis.md`), tokenomics + economy design, trust & safety (anti-sybil at scale), expanded content studio, regional/compliance counsel for geofencing.

## Phase 3 — Ecosystem Dominance (≈ Months 15+)

**Primary goal.** Become **infrastructure** — the default status game of Solana, where distribution arbitrage means CAC trends toward zero because status, content, and speculation do the selling. The flywheel runs on its own deposited assets.

**Target audience / persona.** The entire Solana ecosystem and inbound from adjacent cultures (NFT-gaming, memecoin communities) — pulled in by partner rails and viral gravity rather than recruited.

**Loops to IGNITE (sequence).** All seven at full power and compounding. The emphasis shifts to **deepening Ecosystem (7)** and **Community (3, 4)** — partner integrations multiply, the Council/Chamber social fabric becomes self-governing lore.

**Channels.** Ecosystem and community become the dominant channels; paid trends toward zero as a share of new Operators (per `../01_acquisition/channel_strategy.md`). Activate P3 partners (Tensor, Magic Eden, Bonk/memecoin crossovers — brand-gated).

**Headline tactics.**
- **Status-object economy** — Council insignia and season trophies as tradeable on-chain prestige objects (Tensor/Magic Eden).
- **Memecoin-community crossovers** as curated spectacle *events*, within hard premium guardrails — capturing reach without adopting the register.
- **Self-sustaining season cadence** with Grand Operator lore as durable mythology.
- **Partner-embedded Syndicate** — Blinks and surfaces live across wallets, DeFi, and quest platforms by default.

**KPIs / exit criteria.**
- Paid/partner a small minority of new Operators (distribution arbitrage realized).
- Net-negative effective CAC trend (clips/Houses/Council recruit faster than spend).
- Durable W4 retention and a stable, scarce Council with constant turnover drama.
- This phase has no "exit" — it is the steady state of ecosystem gravity.

**Ops / headcount.** Mature org: standing partnerships, growth-ops, economy, trust & safety, content studio, and community/lore teams. Governance of the Council and seasons becomes partly community-run.

## One line

Sequence the loops, never light them all at once: prove the reveal (Clip + KOL), then make Operators recruit (House + Status + Chamber), *then* turn on points and ecosystem at scale on proven retention, and finally let distribution arbitrage make Syndicate the default status game of Solana. Risks to this arc — and their mitigations — are catalogued in `risks_and_mitigations.md`.

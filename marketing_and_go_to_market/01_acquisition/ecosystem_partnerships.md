# Ecosystem Partnerships — Acquisition-Driving Integrations

This is the **Ecosystem / Distribution Loop (7)** as an *acquisition* instrument: integrate where Solana users already transact, route them into Syndicate via Blinks and cross-quests, and let those new Operators bring clips and Houses that make Syndicate worth partnering with — which earns more partners (`../00_foundation/growth_model_and_flywheel.md`).

**Scope discipline:** this doc covers partnerships that *drive new Operators*. The build sequencing, BD process, legal, and integration roadmap live in `../06_ecosystem_distribution/partnerships_pipeline.md` — this is the acquisition lens; that is the operating pipeline. We name targets, the value exchange, and one concrete integration concept each.

## The mutual-value frame

We never ask a partner for "a shoutout." Every integration is a **two-sided exchange**: the partner gets something their users want (a new premium surface, a co-marketing moment, fresh on-chain volume, an airdrop-meta hook); Syndicate gets a borrowed, high-intent, wallet-ready audience. If we can't articulate the partner's win in one line, it isn't a partnership — it's a favor, and favors don't scale.

## Category 1 — Wallets (the rail)

The default surface for Blinks/Actions; the closest thing to "distribution as a primitive" on Solana.

- **Phantom** — the mass-market wallet.
  - *Their win:* a premium, status-driven Blink experience that showcases Actions to their base; differentiated content vs. casino dApps.
  - *Syndicate win:* one-tap "Join a House" from inside the largest wallet audience.
  - *Concept:* **featured Blink placement** — Syndicate reveal cards rendered as native Actions; a curated "join a table tonight" Blink surfaced in Phantom's Explore/Actions surface during exhibition events.
- **Backpack** — the trader-native, exchange-adjacent wallet; sharper audience.
  - *Their win:* exclusive premium content for their power-user base; an event to rally around.
  - *Syndicate win:* direct line to the exact Sharp Trader persona.
  - *Concept:* **Backpack-exclusive Genesis exhibition** + a Blink launched first in Backpack — a co-marketed "the financial sport, first seats here" moment.

## Category 2 — Oracles (the proof + a credibility partner)

Pricing the live market that Operators seal against. **Switchboard is our default oracle**; Pyth is a co-marketing and redundancy partner.

- **Switchboard (default)** — feeds the live price that settles every match.
  - *Their win:* a flagship, consumer-facing, provably-fair use case demonstrating their feeds under real stakes — exactly the showcase oracles want.
  - *Syndicate win:* the integrity backbone of "provably fair," plus co-marketing to the builder/DeFi audience that trusts them.
  - *Concept:* **"Settled on Switchboard" co-branding** on every reveal card and settlement page + a joint thread on the oracle mechanics (pairs with the mechanics deep-dive, `crypto_twitter_playbook.md`).
- **Pyth** — broad price-feed network with a large, points-aware community.
  - *Their win:* additional consumer surface; community cross-pollination.
  - *Syndicate win:* reach into Pyth's large ecosystem audience; oracle redundancy story.
  - *Concept:* **co-marketing + cross-quest** tied to Pyth's community programs; redundancy framing strengthens our fairness narrative.

## Category 3 — Perps / DeFi (the highest-fit audience)

These users already take directional risk, hold USDC, and respect a fair game. They convert into the Sharp Trader persona at the best rate of any pool — and their KOLs are our top sourcing pool (`kol_and_creator_program.md`).

- **Drift** — the perps DEX; our most aligned audience.
  - *Their win:* a status/entertainment layer for their traders; a reason to engage between funding periods; co-marketed competition.
  - *Syndicate win:* "where Drift traders go to compete with *each other* for status and a pot" (our anti-positioning vs. perps, `../00_foundation/positioning_and_brand.md`).
  - *Concept:* **trading-comp crossover** — Drift competition leaderboard earns seats / entry to a Syndicate exhibition; a "Drift × Syndicate" invitational table.
- **Jupiter** — the aggregator and router; enormous reach, points/LFG culture.
  - *Their win:* a premium Action to feature; alignment with their quest/points machinery.
  - *Syndicate win:* the broadest high-intent funnel on Solana.
  - *Concept:* **Jupiter quest + Blink** — a quest step that lands users on a Syndicate "Join a House" Action; co-marketed during a Jupiter community event.
- **marginfi** — lending/borrowing; points-native, sticky users.
  - *Their win:* a new way to put idle USDC to *competitive* use; cross-community moment.
  - *Syndicate win:* points-aware users primed for our Speculation Loop (5).
  - *Concept:* **cross-quest** between marginfi points actions and a first Syndicate match.
- **Kamino** — vaults/lending; yield-and-points sophisticated base.
  - *Their win:* engagement and co-marketing to a high-net-worth-of-attention crowd.
  - *Syndicate win:* a serious, capital-comfortable audience that fits the premium frame.
  - *Concept:* **co-marketed "from yield to the table"** campaign + cross-quest.
- **Zeta** — options/perps; derivatives-literate traders.
  - *Their win:* a complementary skill-expressive product for their risk-takers.
  - *Syndicate win:* users who already think in conviction and position sizing.
  - *Concept:* **joint exhibition + Blink** aimed at the derivatives crowd; "size your conviction" framing.

## Category 4 — NFT / Gaming (the spectacle + status crowd)

Audiences fluent in status objects, collecting, and on-chain identity — native to our Council/Grand Operator status system.

- **Tensor** — the trader-grade NFT marketplace; sharp, competitive, points-aware.
  - *Their win:* a competitive arena for their status-driven users; collectible angle.
  - *Syndicate win:* a crowd that already pays for status and screenshots flexes.
  - *Concept:* **collectible Council/coronation cards** (commemorative, status-marking — *earned*, never a paid rank) + cross-promo with Tensor; the reveal card as a luxury object meets their collecting culture.
- **Magic Eden** — the mass NFT/gaming marketplace; broad reach + launchpad surface.
  - *Their win:* premium game content; a marquee event for their gaming push.
  - *Syndicate win:* large status-fluent audience and a discovery surface.
  - *Concept:* **co-marketed launch feature** + Blink placement on their gaming/discovery surface during the public phase (`launch_campaign.md`).

## Category 5 — Points / Quest platforms (mercenary attention → real play)

Convert airdrop-meta attention into matches. Because Syndicate points are earned by *playing and retaining* (`../05_economy_incentives/`), quest traffic that lands here turns into real activity, not farm-and-bounce.

- **Quest / points-meta platforms** (the campaign hubs traders already grind).
  - *Their win:* a high-quality, premium quest that retains — better completion-to-retention than typical farm quests.
  - *Syndicate win:* the airdrop-farmer persona funneled into a first match via a quest step.
  - *Concept:* a **multi-step quest** ending in "play your first contested match," rewarded in Syndicate points — the Speculation Loop (5) ignition.

## Category 6 — Infra (the rails that make it one-tap)

The plumbing of Solana-native distribution. These aren't audience partners; they're the **mechanism** the other five categories ride on.

- **Dialect / Blinks (Actions)** — the standard for one-tap on-chain CTAs in tweets and apps.
  - *Their win:* a flagship premium Blinks use case (recruiting + match entry) to showcase the standard.
  - *Syndicate win:* the K-factor rail itself — "Join my House" in one tap (`channel_strategy.md`, `crypto_twitter_playbook.md`).
  - *Concept:* **co-developed reveal-card + House Blink** as a reference implementation; joint "what great Actions look like" co-marketing.
- **Helius** — RPC/indexing/webhooks infra.
  - *Their win:* a high-visibility consumer app on their stack; a case study.
  - *Syndicate win:* the reliability and event-indexing that powers real-time reveal clips and on-chain settlement display.
  - *Concept:* **infra partnership + case study** co-marketed to the Solana builder audience (credibility halo, secondary acquisition).

## Partnership tiers

| Tier | Who | What we co-invest | What we get |
|------|-----|-------------------|-------------|
| **Strategic / Anchor** | Default oracle (Switchboard), Blinks rail (Dialect), 1–2 marquee DeFi (Drift, Jupiter) | Deep integration, co-branding, joint events, roadmap alignment | Core rails + flagship co-marketed audiences |
| **Co-marketing** | Other DeFi (marginfi, Kamino, Zeta), wallets (Phantom, Backpack), NFT (Tensor, Magic Eden), Pyth | Cross-quests, Blink placements, joint threads/events | Borrowed high-intent audiences |
| **Quest / Tactical** | Points/quest platforms, smaller communities | A premium quest + reward integration | Funneled farmer attention that retains |
| **Infra** | Helius + supporting infra | Integration + a case study | Reliability + builder-audience credibility |

## Outreach approach

- **Lead with their user's win, not ours.** The first line is what *their* audience gets — a premium Action, a status arena, a co-marketed event — never "please promote us."
- **Bring the asset, not a pitch.** Show up with a working Blink, a reveal card, an exhibition concept. Partners say yes to a thing they can see, especially during a co-marketed event window.
- **Anchor first, then fan out.** Lock the strategic rails (Switchboard, Dialect/Blinks) and 1–2 marquee DeFi names; their logos de-risk every subsequent co-marketing conversation.
- **Time to the launch beats.** Sequence announcements to the launch calendar (`launch_campaign.md`) so each partner moment is also an acquisition spike, not an isolated press note.
- **Hand off to the pipeline.** Once a partner is warm, BD execution, legal/geofencing posture, and build sequencing move to `../06_ecosystem_distribution/partnerships_pipeline.md`. This doc's job ends at "this partnership will drive Operators, and here's the concept."

## The one line

Don't rebuild Phantom, Switchboard, or Jupiter's distribution — **integrate into it.** Every partner is a borrowed audience plus a co-marketed moment, routed in one tap via Blinks, converting into Operators who then make Syndicate the partner everyone else wants.

# Channel Strategy — The Acquisition Portfolio

Acquisition is not a media-buying exercise. It is the act of **lighting loops**. The flywheel (`../00_foundation/growth_model_and_flywheel.md`) does the compounding; channels exist only to inject the first Operators, the first clips, the first Houses into a loop that then runs on status and self-interest.

## The governing principle: every dollar buys a loop, not a click

A click is a terminal asset — you pay, you get a visit, the value ends. A **loop** is a durable asset — you pay once and it deposits clips, Houses, Council storylines, and points gravity that keep recruiting for free (see "Why it compounds," flywheel doc).

So every channel is judged on one question: **what does it leave behind after the spend stops?**

- A paid CT ad that drives waitlist signups → leaves nothing. **Reject.**
- The same budget given to a KOL as House upside → leaves a House that recruits, clips that circulate, a Council storyline. **Fund.**
- A Blink CTA in an Operator's tweet costs us ~zero and deposits a one-tap on-chain recruiting rail. **Maximize.**

We do not optimize CAC in isolation. We optimize **CAC ÷ loop-assets-deposited**. A channel with 2× the CAC that seeds the Clip Loop beats a cheap channel that seeds nothing.

## Channel = persona × loop

Each channel is mapped to a persona from `../00_foundation/target_segments_personas.md` and the single loop it is built to ignite. A channel that doesn't ignite a loop is not a channel — it's a leak.

### Tier 1 — Owned top-of-funnel (the engine)

- **Crypto Twitter (CT), organic** — *primary* top-of-funnel. Persona: the Sharp Trader and the Status Climber. Loop: **Clip (1)** + **Status/Council (3)**. CT is where reveal clips, Council movements, and Grand Operator lore live and circulate. Effectively zero marginal cost; the asset deposited is attention compounding on prior attention. Full doctrine: `crypto_twitter_playbook.md`.
- **Blinks / Actions** — the conversion *rail*, not a top-of-funnel. Persona: anyone already mid-scroll on CT. Loop: **House referral (2)**. A one-tap "join my House" Action embedded in an Operator's tweet collapses discovery→wallet→table into a single tap. This is the K-factor multiplier — it raises *conversion per invite* in the growth equation. Cost: near-zero. Asset deposited: a frictionless recruiting surface attached to every Operator.

### Tier 2 — Leveraged human distribution

- **KOL-as-Operator** — leverage, not media. Persona: the KOL's *audience* (mixed trader/degenerate/status). Loop: **KOL-as-Operator (6)**, which feeds **Clip (1)** and **House (2)**. We do not rent a post; we make the KOL an Operator with a House and upside, so their status compounds when their audience plays. Full program: `kol_and_creator_program.md`.
- **Streamer co-stream** — live spectacle. Persona: the spectator who hasn't played. Loop: **Clip (1)** + **Status (3)**. A high-stakes exhibition match streamed turns a passive viewer into someone who wants the seat. Asset deposited: VOD + clips that outlive the stream.

### Tier 3 — Ecosystem rails (borrowed audiences)

- **Wallet / DeFi / oracle partnerships** — borrowed distribution. Persona: the Sharp Trader already on Drift, Jupiter, marginfi, Kamino, Zeta; the wallet-native on Phantom/Backpack. Loop: **Ecosystem (7)**. We meet Solana users where they already transact and route them in via Blinks and cross-quests. Full target list and integration concepts: `ecosystem_partnerships.md`; the build/sequence pipeline lives in `../06_ecosystem_distribution/partnerships_pipeline.md`.
- **Points / quest platforms** — points-meta gravity. Persona: the airdrop farmer / mercenary capital. Loop: **Speculation/Points (5)** + **Ecosystem (7)**. Quests on the points-meta platforms convert farmer attention into actual matches; because our points are earned by *playing and retaining* (by design, see `../05_economy_incentives/`), the mercenary energy converts into real activity.

### Tier 4 — Community-owned (compounding for free)

- **Discord / Telegram (House + Chamber homes)** — retention-adjacent acquisition. Persona: the already-converted Operator recruiting peers. Loop: **House (2)** + **Chamber (4)**. Not a top-of-funnel; it is where recruiting *happens* once the loop is lit. Detail: `../04_community/`.
- **Council rituals & coronations** — earned-media spectacle. Persona: aspirational climbers + spectators. Loop: **Status/Council (3)** + **Clip (1)**. A coronation broadcast is a free acquisition event because status is inherently shareable.

### Explicitly de-prioritized (Phase 0–2)

- **Paid search / Google / app-store ASO** — wrong frame. There is no high-intent search demand for a category we are *creating*. Buys clicks, deposits nothing.
- **Generic paid social (Meta/TikTok ads)** — off-brand and off-audience. Reads as casino acquisition; repels the competitor we want; the Solana-native audience lives on CT, not the feed. Revisit only post-PMF if a *premium* spectator-content format proves out.
- **Influencer pay-per-post** — banned by doctrine. Flat-fee posts attract mercenaries and produce one terminal impression. We convert KOLs to Operators with upside instead (see `kol_and_creator_program.md`).

## Budget allocation logic by phase

Allocation follows the loop-sequencing rule from the flywheel: **don't light all loops at once.** Spend goes where the *next* loop in sequence needs a spark.

| Phase | What we're proving | Where the budget goes | Why |
|-------|--------------------|-----------------------|-----|
| **0 — Stealth / Genesis** | Reveal sells; culture exists | ~70% Founding KOLs (House upside) · ~20% exhibition/stream production · ~10% CT content production | Seed Clip + KOL loops with the *right* faces before anyone can buy in. No paid acquisition — only loop-seeding. |
| **1 — Whitelist / Invite waves** | Operators become recruiters | ~45% KOL/creator program (broaden) · ~25% Blinks/referral tooling & incentives · ~20% community ops (Houses/Chambers) · ~10% content | Turn retained players into Houses; fund the K-factor rail, not impressions. |
| **2 — Public** | Loops self-sustain; ecosystem scales | ~35% ecosystem partnerships & co-marketing · ~25% points/quest campaigns · ~20% KOL renewals/new tiers · ~20% content + rituals | Speculation + Ecosystem loops come on at full power *only now*, landing on proven retention, not vaporware. |
| **3 — Dominance** | Distribution arbitrage | Shift to ecosystem + community; paid trends toward zero as a share of new Operators | Status, content, and speculation do the selling. CAC falls every loop turn. |

The north-star check for whether the spend is working is unchanged from the flywheel: **Weekly Active Operators who reach a contested reveal**, **K-factor**, and **W4 retention** rising together (`../07_growth_ops_metrics/north_star_and_kpis.md`).

## Build vs. rent

| Capability | Decision | Rationale |
|------------|----------|-----------|
| Top-of-funnel narrative (CT presence, lore, rituals) | **Build** | This *is* the brand and the cheapest acquisition. Never outsource the voice. |
| Clip / reveal-card generation | **Build** | The Clip Engine is core IP and the flagship loop (`../03_virality_network_effects/spectator_and_clip_engine.md`). Beauty + status is our distribution; it cannot be rented. |
| Referral rails (Blinks, House invite logic) | **Build** | K-factor is the growth equation. Owning the rail lets us tune conversion-per-invite. |
| Audience reach | **Rent → convert** | Rent KOL/partner reach to *seed* loops, but convert it to ownership (KOLs become Operators with Houses; partners get durable integrations). |
| Wallet / oracle / DeFi surfaces | **Partner** | Don't rebuild Phantom or Switchboard's distribution; integrate via Blinks/quests (`ecosystem_partnerships.md`). |
| Ad platforms / paid clicks | **Neither** | Doesn't deposit a loop asset. Avoid. |
| Streaming / production | **Rent (services)** | Production is a vendor; the spectacle and IP are ours. |

The rule: **build the loops, rent the spark, never rent the user.**

## One line

We do not buy users. We buy the *first turn* of a loop that then recruits the rest for free. Judge every channel by the loop it leaves behind — and when in doubt, fund the one that deposits a clip, a House, or a Council storyline.

# Crypto Twitter Playbook — The Primary Top-of-Funnel

CT is where Syndicate lives. It is where the Sharp Trader scrolls between Drift fills, where the Status Climber tracks who's ascending, where memes are minted and reputations made. Our top-of-funnel doctrine (`channel_strategy.md`) names CT Tier 1 because it is the cheapest, highest-leverage surface we have: **attention compounds on prior attention, and every post deposits a loop asset.**

This doc is the operating manual for the Syndicate account *and* for the voice we want every Operator to imitate. The brand voice is defined in `../00_foundation/positioning_and_brand.md` — **confident, terse, insider, restraint-as-flex, numbers are sacred, no emoji-spam, no hype.** We are the account that *doesn't* shill, which is exactly why we read as the premium one on the timeline.

## The strategic role of each post

Every post serves a loop, never just "engagement":

- **Reveal clips** → Clip Loop (1). The flagship. (`../03_virality_network_effects/spectator_and_clip_engine.md`)
- **Council movements** → Status/Council Loop (3). Manufactured drama; aspiration.
- **Grand Operator lore** → Status (3) + brand prestige.
- **Mechanics deep-dives** → activation; convert lurkers who need to *understand* before they enter.
- **Honest-skill takes** → brand moat; repel the lottery churner, attract the competitor.

If a draft serves none of these, it doesn't ship.

## Posting cadence

- **Daily:** 1 reveal clip (the hero), 1–2 reply/quote interactions in high-signal trader/poker threads.
- **3×/week:** a mechanics or honest-skill thread.
- **Weekly ritual — Sunday:** **The Council Update** thread (movements, ascensions, the seat-changes of the week).
- **Event-driven:** coronation broadcasts, exhibition matches, Council inductions — these are the spikes the cadence exists to support.

Restraint is the cadence rule too: **fewer, heavier posts beat a noisy feed.** A premium account does not post 10× a day.

## Threads vs. cards — when to use what

- **Card** = the default and the majority. One artifact, one line, one Blink. Maximum signal, minimum scroll.
- **Thread** = reserved for rituals (Council Update), teaching (mechanics), and lore. Threads must front-load the payoff in tweet 1 — premium voice never makes you work for the point.

## Seeding and riding memes — within premium guardrails

Memecoin culture (Bonk, the broader CT meme cycle) is the water CT swims in. We do not pretend to be above it; we participate **on our own terms.**

- **Mint our own in-group vocabulary, not jokes.** "The table remembers." "Sealed." "A seat opened." "The Council moved." These are memes that confer status when repeated — the lexicon *is* the meme (`../00_foundation/lexicon_and_status_system.md`).
- **Ride a trend only when we can land it dry.** When CT is mid-frenzy on a price move, the move is: a Reveal Card of an Operator who sealed that exact level. We don't make the joke; we *are* the punchline, delivered straight.
- **Never:** rocket emojis, "wen," "gm" spam as primary content, jackpot/casino framing, or chasing a meme that forces us to break the institutional register. The flex is that we don't chase.
- **The Grand Operator as meme-able myth.** One apex seat, lore around the holder, defended-days counter — this is inherently meme-generative without a single joke.

## Quote-tweet and raid etiquette

- **Quote-tweet to elevate, not to dunk.** When an Operator posts a great reveal, the official account QTs with one line of gravity ("This is the game."). We amplify members; we don't punch down at competitors or rival projects.
- **No coordinated raids, no engagement-farming pods.** They read as desperate and off-brand, and they attract exactly the mercenary energy we filter out. Our "raid" is a coronation everyone *wants* to share.
- **Reply where the sharps already are.** Thoughtful, terse replies in serious trader and poker threads — adding a real read, never a CTA — earns the follow of the persona we want. One sharp reply > ten generic ones.
- **Defend the brand, calmly.** If accused of being a casino, answer with the mechanism (Example 3), not defensiveness. The honest-skill posture is a moat; use it.

## Blinks in tweets — the one-tap House CTA

Blinks/Actions are how a tweet becomes a recruiting rail (the House referral loop, K-factor — see `channel_strategy.md` and `../03_virality_network_effects/chambers_and_houses.md`).

- **Every Reveal Card ends with a Blink.** Default: a "Join a House" Action that opens wallet → seat in one tap, no leaving the timeline.
- **Operator-personalized Blinks.** An Operator's own tweet carries *their* House Blink — "Join my House" — so their distribution directly fills their table and credits their House. This is the line where a post stops being content and becomes a channel.
- **Coronation Blinks.** During a coronation broadcast, the pinned tweet's Blink routes to the next exhibition's waitlist/seat. Spectacle → one tap → Operator.

Example CTA copy (kept dry):

> A seat opens at this table tonight. Two minutes to learn it in practice. ↓ [Blink: Join my House]

## The rituals (non-negotiable, recurring)

Rituals are the heartbeat that makes CT feel like a living institution, not a marketing feed:

1. **Sunday — The Council Update thread.** Movements, ascensions, falls. (Status loop.)
2. **Coronation broadcast.** When a Grand Operator is crowned or defends, the account runs a live thread + clip finale. The first coronation is a launch finale beat (`launch_campaign.md`).
3. **Genesis / induction notes.** When a new Operator enters The Council, a single dignified card marks it. Earned, never bought — protecting the skill brand (`kol_and_creator_program.md`).
4. **The defended-days counter.** A recurring lore beat on the current Grand Operator's reign — myth in motion.

## What "good" looks like (the bar)

- A non-Operator screenshots a Reveal Card *because it looks expensive.*
- A trader QTs a mechanics thread saying "ok this is actually fair, unlike X."
- An Operator's personal House Blink fills their table without us spending a cent.
- The lexicon shows up in the wild — strangers saying "the table remembers" — because the in-group language went meme.

When those happen, CT is doing its job: seeding the Clip and House loops at near-zero cost, on a premium register no casino can imitate.

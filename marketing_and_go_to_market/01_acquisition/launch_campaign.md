# Launch Campaign — Genesis to Public

The launch is not an announcement; it is the **first turn of the flywheel, performed in public.** Its job is to light the right loops in the right order (`../00_foundation/growth_model_and_flywheel.md`): the **Clip Loop (1)**, **House Loop (2)**, and **KOL-as-Operator Loop (6)** first — *not* speculation or paid scale. We light culture before we light incentives.

The texture throughout is **invite-only and earned** (`../00_foundation/positioning_and_brand.md`): admission feels like being let into something serious. Scarcity is the marketing.

## The narrative arc

```
STEALTH  →  GENESIS COUNCIL  →  INVITE WAVES  →  PUBLIC
(intrigue)   (founding Operators)  (controlled scarcity)  (the doors open)
```

- **Stealth** — presence without product. A premium account, a lexicon, fragments of lore. Demand is built by *withholding*.
- **Genesis Council** — the founding Operators: Founding KOLs (`kol_and_creator_program.md`) + hand-picked sharp early players. The first faces and the first Houses.
- **Invite waves** — whitelist expands in deliberate waves, each Operator handed a small number of invites. The invite *is* the House loop's first fuel.
- **Public** — doors open, ecosystem + points loops come on at full power, landing on proven retention.

## Phase beats

### Phase S — Stealth (intrigue without explanation)

- Launch the CT account in full premium voice. No product link. Post lexicon and lore fragments — "The Council is forming." "A seat is not given." — that mean nothing yet and therefore mean everything.
- Stand up a **waitlist** behind a single dignified page. No feature list; a register, a sealed aesthetic, a counter.
- Seed curiosity in sharp trader and poker circles via 1:1 invitation, not broadcast. Let CT ask "what is Syndicate?" — and don't fully answer.
- Lock the **strategic partner rails** quietly (Switchboard as default oracle, Dialect/Blinks, 1–2 marquee DeFi) so they're ready to co-market on cue (`ecosystem_partnerships.md`).

### Phase G — Genesis Council (the founding Operators)

- **Reveal the mechanic** with the first mechanics deep-dive thread (template C, `crypto_twitter_playbook.md`) — now the lore has a game under it.
- Admit the **Genesis Council**: Founding KOLs + selected sharps get the first seats and found the first **Houses**. This is the KOL loop ignition and the seed of the recruiting graph.
- Open **practice (no-stakes) mode** to the whitelist so the "aha" — a first SEALED→REVEAL — happens in minutes, before any real buy-in (the Activate step of the flywheel).
- Run the first private real-money tables (tiers, fresh per-match buy-in, geofenced). The **Clip Engine** starts producing reveal cards immediately — the first clips enter the wild.

### Phase W — Invite waves (controlled scarcity → K-factor)

- Hand every Genesis Operator a **small allotment of invites** (e.g., 3–5), delivered as a **personal House Blink** — one-tap "join my House" (`channel_strategy.md`). Scarcity makes each invite a status object; the Blink makes redeeming it frictionless.
- Expand the whitelist in **named waves** ("Wave II opens Thursday"), each a CT moment. The line of people *wanting* in is itself the campaign.
- **Featured Operators** (`kol_and_creator_program.md`) come online here, broadening the recruiting graph beyond the Founding KOLs.
- Begin the **Sunday Council Update** ritual — now there are real movements to report, manufacturing the first drama.

### Phase P — Public (doors open, loops at full power)

- Remove the whitelist gate; keep the *texture* (Houses, earned rank, invite culture remain).
- Turn on **ecosystem acquisition**: cross-quests and Blink placements with wallets/DeFi/NFT partners (`ecosystem_partnerships.md`) and the **points/quest** campaigns — the Speculation Loop (5) and Ecosystem Loop (7) now land on a product with proven retention, not vaporware.
- The public phase is anchored by the **first Grand Operator coronation** (below) as its finale spectacle.

## The drama engine: exhibition + coronation

Manufactured, *honest* drama is the launch's gravity well. Two set-pieces:

1. **The public high-stakes exhibition match (streamed).** A marquee table — Founding KOLs + a poker creator (`kol_and_creator_program.md`) — seated at high stakes and broadcast (co-streamed across creator channels). Real conviction, sealed live, revealed live, a real pot. It is the single best advertisement we have: it *shows* the financial sport instead of describing it, and it mints a stack of reveal clips. Pinned tweet carries a Blink to the next wave's seats.
2. **The first Grand Operator coronation (the finale).** The public phase climaxes with the crowning of the first **Grand Operator** — the apex seat, earned at the table, never bestowed. Co-streamed, threaded, clipped; a defended-days counter begins the same hour. This is the Status Loop (3) detonating: one seat, permanent chase, infinitely shareable. It is the emotional close of the launch and the open of the season-long story.

Both are honest by construction — real stakes, on-chain settlement, provable fairness — which is exactly why they're more compelling than any casino's manufactured hype.

## Waitlist + Blinks mechanics

The waitlist and Blinks are the campaign's plumbing, tuned to feed the House loop:

- **Waitlist** = a status ladder, not a form. Position is visible; referring others (via your eventual House) moves you up. Scarcity + a climbable number = built-in virality before the product even opens.
- **Genesis admission** by selection (KOLs + sharps), not first-come — protecting quality and the earned-status brand.
- **Invite Blinks** = the conversion rail. Each Genesis/Featured Operator's invites are minted as personal "Join my House" Actions; tapping opens wallet → seat without leaving the timeline. This is where a tweet becomes a channel and K compounds.
- **Event Blinks** = exhibition and coronation tweets route, in one tap, to the next available wave/table — turning spectacle directly into Operators.

## Founding-Operator perks (earned-status-safe)

What Genesis Operators get for being first — none of which is a purchased rank:

- A **named, featured House** and top House points/rev share (`kol_and_creator_program.md`).
- A permanent **"Genesis" mark** on their Operator profile — commemorative status for being early, not a leaderboard shortcut.
- **First invites** (the scarce, status-bearing allotment) and earliest access to new tiers/features.
- A **Council fast-lane** = early access to the table and early visibility — *the rank itself is still earned at the table.* (Non-negotiable line, `kol_and_creator_program.md`.)
- A role in the **Genesis narrative** — their early reveals and ascensions become the founding lore the brand retells.

The perks reward *being early and recruiting*, never *paying for status* — keeping faith with the skill brand even at the most hype-prone moment.

## How launch ignites the first loops (and which it withholds)

| Loop | Lit at launch? | How |
|------|:---:|-----|
| **Clip (1)** | **First** | Genesis matches + the streamed exhibition produce reveal clips immediately |
| **House (2)** | **First** | Invite allotments delivered as personal House Blinks → K-factor from day one |
| **KOL (6)** | **First** | Founding KOLs *are* the Genesis Council; their Houses seed the recruiting graph |
| **Status / Council (3)** | Phase W → P | Sunday Council Updates, then the coronation finale |
| **Chamber (4)** | Phase P | Teams form once there's a populated ladder to compete on |
| **Speculation / Points (5)** | **Withheld → Phase P** | Points/quest campaigns only after retention is proven — never lead with mercenary energy |
| **Ecosystem (7)** | Rails locked in Stealth; **activated Phase P** | Cross-quests/Blinks with partners go live at public scale |

The discipline that makes this work: **we light culture (Clip, House, KOL) before incentives (Speculation, Ecosystem).** Virality before retention burns the brand; speculation before a real product is vaporware. Genesis proves the reveal sells and the Houses recruit; only then do we pour on scale.

## The one line

Launch by *withholding*, admit the right faces first, hand them scarce invites as one-tap House Blinks, manufacture honest drama with a streamed exhibition, and crown the first Grand Operator as the finale — so that by the time the doors are fully open, the table is already full and the loops are already turning.

# Rituals, Events & Seasons — the calendar of habit and content

Status is the engine; **the calendar is the flywheel's clock.** Rituals convert one-time players into Operators with a *standing daily habit*, and every ritual is engineered to spit out a **shareable artifact** — a clip, a leaderboard delta, a coronation — that recruits the next Operator. Nothing on this calendar exists only to entertain insiders; each event is simultaneously a retention loop and an acquisition asset (`../00_foundation/growth_model_and_flywheel.md`).

## Design principle: every ritual is an artifact factory

For each ritual we pre-answer three questions, or it doesn't ship:

1. **What habit does it build?** (the retention job — a reason to return on a known cadence)
2. **What standing does it move?** (the status job — it must touch rank, Council, Chamber, or House)
3. **What artifact does it emit?** (the acquisition job — the clip/card/board that performs on CT)

```
RITUAL ──▶ STANDING MOVES ──▶ ARTIFACT EMITTED ──▶ POSTED FOR STATUS ──▶ NEW OPERATOR
   ▲                                                                          │
   └───────────────────── returns on the next cadence ◀───────────────────────┘
```

## Cadence table

| Ritual | Cadence | Touches | Artifact emitted |
|--------|---------|---------|------------------|
| **The Daily Seal** | daily | Operator rating, daily board | "Hand of the Day" reveal clip |
| **Reveal of the Day** | daily | Floor culture | auto-clip in `#the-reveal-room` |
| **Council Watch** | ~daily | The Council board | Council delta card ("X entered / Y dethroned") |
| **Weekly Open** | weekly (fixed slot) | season rating, weekly leaderboard | weekly winners card, top-table clips |
| **Chamber War** | weekly / bi-weekly | Chamber standings | war scoreboard, decisive-hand clip |
| **House Night** | weekly | House internal boards, founder share | House crest + winner card |
| **Exhibition Main Event** | monthly | spotlight ranks, lore | broadcast VOD, headline reveal clip |
| **Season Climax + Coronation** | per season (~90 days) | The Council, Grand Operator | coronation broadcast, Hall-of-Champions entry |
| **Council Summit** | per season | Council belonging, governance | scarce IRL/virtual photos, proposal recap |

The cadence is deliberately layered: **daily** for habit, **weekly** for rhythm and team stakes, **monthly** for spectacle, **seasonal** for the grand arc. An Operator should always have something contested within 24 hours *and* a season-long climb to defend.

## The weekly heartbeat

A predictable week is what manufactures habit. Slots are fixed and announced as institution events, not casual play:

```
MON  Season standings posted · "the week opens" · Council Watch
WED  CHAMBER WAR — scheduled Chamber-vs-Chamber bracket
FRI  THE WEEKLY OPEN — flagship open tournament, prime CT hours
SAT  HOUSE NIGHT — founders run private-table events for their crews
SUN  Week-in-review: top reveals, leaderboard deltas, "Read of the Week"
```

## Event formats (concrete)

### The Weekly Open (flagship tournament)
- **Format:** open-entry, multi-table, escalating stakes; closest-eligible-prediction-wins resolves each table; survivors advance to a final table.
- **Stakes:** USDC buy-in with a season-points overlay; final table is broadcast.
- **Artifact:** a winners card, a "final-table reveal" clip, and a leaderboard delta. The final table is the week's hero content.
- **Why it retains:** a fixed, must-attend slot in prime CT hours becomes a weekly appointment; rating on the line keeps the climb live.

### Chamber War (team sport)
- **Format:** two Chambers, a scheduled bracket of head-to-head tables; aggregate result moves both Chambers' season standing. Rivalries are seeded and storylined.
- **Stakes:** Chamber standing + a war trophy + bragging rights in `#leaderboards`.
- **Artifact:** a war scoreboard and the decisive-hand clip — *teams* posting for *team* status multiplies reach (`./ambassador_and_chamber_program.md`).
- **Why it retains:** you don't no-show on your Chamber — social obligation is the stickiest retention lever there is.

### House Night (the invite loop, ritualized)
- **Format:** House Founders run private-table events for their members; internal House leaderboards; founder earns House points/rev share on activity.
- **Stakes:** House standing, founder upside, an internal champion.
- **Artifact:** House crest + winner card — gives founders a *reason and a moment* to recruit and fill tables (`../03_virality_network_effects/chambers_and_houses.md`).

### Exhibition Main Event (broadcast spectacle)
- **Format:** an invited, curated table — Council members, the reigning Grand Operator, marquee KOL-Operators, or a storyline grudge match — played live and cast with commentary.
- **Stakes:** prestige and lore, not just a pot. The Grand Operator may seed the matchup (`./the_council_and_grand_operator.md`).
- **Artifact:** a full broadcast VOD and a headline reveal clip — the monthly tentpole that pulls non-members in (`../01_acquisition/kol_and_creator_program.md`, `../03_virality_network_effects/spectator_and_clip_engine.md`).

## Seasons — the grand arc

A **season (~90 days)** is the container that gives the whole ladder stakes and a reset. Without seasons, ranks calcify and the climb dies; with them, status is always *rented from current form*, and every season is a fresh acquisition + reactivation event.

### Anatomy of a season
```
WEEK 1        SEASON OPEN  — board resets; "the climb reopens"; new map/asset rotation
WEEKS 2–11    THE CLIMB    — weekly heartbeat runs; Council board churns daily
WEEK ~10      THE RECKONING— Council seats lock into contention; dethroning drama peaks
WEEK 12       SEASON CLIMAX— playoffs / throne match
              CORONATION   — Grand Operator crowned (broadcast ritual)
              COUNCIL SUMMIT— elite retro + next-season preview
BREAK         RESET        — Hall of Champions updated; board zeroes; reactivation push
```

### Sample Season 3 calendar (illustrative)

| Phase | Dates (example) | Marquee moments |
|-------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Open | Wk 1 | Season-open broadcast; new asset rotation; Council board resets to season ratings |
| Climb | Wk 2–6 | Weekly Opens; Chamber Wars; first big dethronings; KOL exhibition |
| Mid-season | Wk 7 | Mid-Season Exhibition Main Event; Chamber playoffs seeded |
| Stretch | Wk 8–11 | Council Watch intensifies; "contested seat" watches; House Nights ramp |
| Climax | Wk 12 | Throne Match → **Coronation** → **Council Summit** → board reset |

Seasonal cadence syncs with the points/airdrop meta so a new season is also an economic reactivation moment (`../05_economy_incentives/`).

## How rituals compound retention and acquisition

- **Daily** rituals build the *habit* that floors retention — there is always a contested thing today.
- **Weekly** rituals add *rhythm and team stakes* (Chamber Wars) that convert solo play into social obligation.
- **Monthly** exhibitions create *tentpole spectacle* — the broadcastable hero content that recruits at scale.
- **Seasonal** arcs deliver *reset + reactivation + mythology* — coronations and summits that refresh the whole flywheel.
- Every tier **emits an artifact** that an Operator posts for status, turning the calendar into a continuous content stream and every event into a recruiting surface (`../00_foundation/growth_model_and_flywheel.md`).

The test for any new event: *does it build a habit, move a standing, and emit a shareable artifact?* Three yeses, or it's just entertainment — and entertainment that doesn't recruit doesn't earn a calendar slot.

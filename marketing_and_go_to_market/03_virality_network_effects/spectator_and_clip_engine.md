# Spectator & Clip Engine — the Flagship Loop

This is the most important growth system Syndicate builds. Every match already manufactures the most shareable asset in crypto — **a dramatic, provably-fair reveal with real money and real reads on the line.** The Clip Engine's job is to capture that drama as an auto-generated, beautiful, screenshot-native artifact the Operator *wants* to post — turning distribution into a *feature of the product*, not a marketing campaign. This is loop #1 of the seven and our **cheapest, highest-volume acquisition** (`../00_foundation/growth_model_and_flywheel.md`).

> We don't ask Operators to market us. We make their proudest moment so beautiful that posting it is the natural reflex. Virality is downstream of beauty + status, not loudness (`../00_foundation/positioning_and_brand.md`).

## Why the reveal is the perfect viral object

- **Inherent drama:** a sealed conviction (time-lock/drand encrypted, unreadable until match end) snapping open is a cliffhanger with a payoff — narrative built into the mechanic.
- **Provable, so it's credible:** on-chain finality means the clip is *receipts*, not a screenshot anyone could fake. Credibility travels on CT.
- **Numbers are sacred:** the pot, the closeness ranking, the rating swing — authority that reads as a trading flex, not a meme.
- **Universal hook for non-players:** "how close was I?" / "what would have happened?" pulls in spectators who've never taken a seat. The drama is legible to railbirds.

## Auto-generated reveal clips & cards (the core artifact)

Every match auto-produces a share-ready set — no editing, no effort. Default share prompt fires on the reveal's afterglow (the activation peak, see `../02_activation_retention/onboarding_and_aha_moment.md`).

| Artifact | What it shows | Primary use |
|----------|---------------|-------------|
| **Reveal clip** | the sealed→open animation, the live market line, closeness ranking, winning eligible read, pot, payout | the CT flagship post |
| **Hand card (static)** | one-image summary: your sealed read, result, rating Δ, insignia | fast screenshot share / TG |
| **"How close was I?" card** | your prediction vs. close, margin, where you placed | the universal hook, even in a loss |
| **"What would have happened" card** | for **folders** — the reveal *as if they'd stayed*, "you folded a winner / dodged a loss" | retention sting + a clip even when you didn't play to the end |

**The folder mechanic is a growth unlock.** Players who fold early are usually dead content in card games. Here, *all numbers open post-match*, so even a folder gets a dramatic "what would have happened" beat — `Held: you'd have taken the pot.` That manufactures shareable drama (and a comeback urge) from the majority of hands, not just the showdowns. It multiplies the content supply several-fold.

**CT-native share copy (auto-suggested, in-voice):**
> `Sealed at [X]. Market closed [Y]. I read it to within [margin]. Pot: [Z] USDC. On-chain.` [ clip ]
>
> (folder) `Folded a winner. The read was right; the nerve wasn't. Next table.` [ what-would-have-happened card ]

Design bar: dark, high-contrast, gold/serif/mono authority — a **luxury object**, screenshot-worthy by default. A clip that isn't beautiful doesn't ship.

## Shareable profile / Council / leaderboard cards

The status surfaces are clips too — the flex artifacts of the progression spine (`../02_activation_retention/progression_and_ranks.md`):

- **Operator profile card** — rank, season stats, signature reveals, insignia, Chamber/House.
- **Council induction card** — "You entered The Council. Top 100." The leaderboard *is* a recruiting poster (`../00_foundation/lexicon_and_status_system.md`).
- **Rank-up / tier card** — recurring flex fuel each time rating crosses a threshold.
- **Coronation card / clip** — the Grand Operator ceremony, the season's headline.
- **Chamber standing card** — team flex that drafts rivals into the Chamber loop.

These feed the **Status / Council loop** in parallel with match clips — content from *being ranked*, not just from *playing a hand*.

## Watch parties & the live-games dashboard

Spectating is the top of the funnel and the habit layer:

- **Live-games dashboard:** browse live tables, filter by stakes/Council presence, "sweat" a sealed hand in real time. A railbird can watch the drama build *before any reveal*.
- **Watch parties around rituals:** **Council Hour** and the **Season Finale** (`../02_activation_retention/retention_engine.md`) are appointment spectacles — concentrated attention that produces the highest-value clips and the most new-Operator pull.
- **Spectatable Houses/Chambers:** a founder can broadcast their House's table; a Chamber its grudge match (`chambers_and_houses.md`) — turning private competition into public content.
- **Railbird → Operator path:** every spectator surface has a one-tap "take a seat" (Blink), so watching converts to playing on the same screen.

## The future spectator prediction market (skin-in-the-game layer)

Phase 2+: let **spectators put skin in the game without a seat** — a market on live matches ("who lands closest," "does this Operator hold or fold"). This converts passive attention into active, retained, *betting* viewers — a viral skin-in-the-game layer on top of the watching habit.

- **Why it's viral:** a spectator with a position *shares the match* to defend their read and recruit fellow bettors — every spectator becomes a distributor of the live game.
- **Why it expands TAM:** the ambient Spectator/Railbird persona (`../00_foundation/target_segments_personas.md`) monetizes and amplifies without ever taking a USDC seat.
- **Guardrails:** must stay inside the anti-casino frame (skill-readable markets, provable settlement, anti-sybil), and respect the compliance/geofencing posture (`../05_economy_incentives/incentive_and_anti_sybil_design.md`). Sequenced *after* retention is proven, so it lands on a real product.

## Distribution mechanics (engineering the spread)

Beauty is necessary, not sufficient — we engineer the spread:

1. **Default share prompt at the peak.** The share CTA appears on the reveal afterglow and on every rank-up/Council moment. Friction to share ≈ zero; one tap to CT/TG with copy pre-filled.
2. **CT-optimized formats.** Aspect ratios and durations tuned for the X timeline; a striking still frame (the sealed→open beat) that reads even before autoplay; alt static card for low-bandwidth/TG.
3. **Embedded Blink on every shared artifact.** A clip isn't just content — it carries a one-tap "take a seat" / "join my House" Action, so a viewer converts *inside the post* (closes into the K-engine, `viral_loops_and_referrals.md`).
4. **Leaderboards as evergreen content.** The Council board and Chamber standings are public, linkable, and update on a cadence — recurring, datable CT beats ("The Council updated").
5. **Creator amplification.** KOL-Operators produce the most-watched hands; their clips seed the loop at scale (`../01_acquisition/kol_and_creator_program.md`).
6. **Watermark = brand + receipts.** Every artifact carries restrained Syndicate branding and the on-chain verifiability cue — free brand impressions that read as credibility, not spam.

| | Paid acquisition | Clip Engine |

| Cost per asset | $ per impression, perpetual spend | ~$0 — auto-generated by every match |
| Volume | capped by budget | scales with matches played (incl. folders) |

| Compounding | resets when spend stops | every clip is a durable asset in the wild |

| **Spectator→seat** | dashboard watchers taking a real seat | **≥ 8%** |

Every clip deposits a durable asset that lowers the cost of the *next* Operator — the compounding mechanism at the heart of the flywheel (`../00_foundation/growth_model_and_flywheel.md`).

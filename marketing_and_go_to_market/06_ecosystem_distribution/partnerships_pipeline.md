# Partnerships Pipeline — the Operational Target List

This is the **build/sequence pipeline**: named targets, what each is worth to us, what we are worth to them, the integration concept, a priority tier, and the first ask. It is the operational complement to `../01_acquisition/ecosystem_partnerships.md` (that doc frames partnerships as *acquisition strategy*; this one is the BD execution sheet) and the channel doctrine in `solana_distribution_playbook.md`.

Two rules govern every row, inherited from `../01_acquisition/channel_strategy.md`:

1. **Don't rebuild what a partner already distributes.** We don't rebuild Phantom's reach or Switchboard's feed — we integrate.
2. **Value exchange must flow both ways or it isn't a partnership.** A logo swap is not a partnership. We give the partner's users a *premium status game*, co-marketing, and shared clips/audience; they give us their rail. Every row states both directions.

## Priority tiers (definition)

- **P0 — launch-critical.** The product or the launch narrative does not work without it. (Oracle, primary wallet.)
- **P1 — flywheel-critical.** Materially accelerates a loop; pursue in Phase 1–2.
- **P2 — scale accelerant.** Adds reach/gravity once retention is proven; Phase 2–3.
- **P3 — opportunistic.** High upside, brand-conditional, or long-lead; engage when the brand can carry it.

## What we offer partners (the standing pitch)

The reusable value proposition we lead every conversation with:

- **Your users get a premium status game.** Not another farm — a financial sport with provable, on-chain reputation (The Council, Grand Operator). It *raises* the prestige of being in your ecosystem.
- **Co-marketing to a high-status audience.** Operators are sharp, vocal, and status-seeking; association with The Council is desirable co-branding, not generic cross-promo.
- **Shared clips and audience.** The Clip Engine (`../03_virality_network_effects/spectator_and_clip_engine.md`) manufactures beautiful, viral reveal moments. A co-branded reveal clip is earned media for the partner, not a paid spot.
- **On-chain credibility.** "Provably fair, settled by [oracle], non-custodial via [wallet]" makes the partner a marquee, named beneficiary of the integration.
- **No casino baggage.** We are explicitly ANTI-CASINO and geofence real-money tiers; partners can co-market without compliance fear (compliance posture: regulation deferred, real-money GEOFENCED, practice mode open).

## Outreach approach

- **Lead with the rail, not the logo.** Open every conversation with the concrete integration concept and the partner's upside ("your users get a premium status game + co-marketed clips"), not a generic "let's partner."
- **Bring a working Blink to the table.** A live "Take a seat" Action is more persuasive than a deck — it proves the integration is one tap from done and showcases the premium frame.
- **Sequence to the flywheel.** Close P0 (oracle, primary wallet, Dialect, Helius) *before* launch; pursue P1 during invite waves; turn on P2 (quests, DeFi cross-promos, points aggregators) only when retention is proven; treat P3 as opportunistic and brand-gated.
- **Convert relationships into Operators.** Where a partner has a face (a founder, a lead), make them an Operator with a House. A partner who *plays* evangelizes for free — the strongest possible integration.
- **Co-marketing is reciprocal clips, not ad swaps.** Every partnership ships at least one co-branded reveal clip; that is the deliverable, because clips are the loop (`../03_virality_network_effects/spectator_and_clip_engine.md`).
- **Compliance is a selling point.** State the posture up front — ANTI-CASINO, real-money GEOFENCED, practice open — so partners can co-market without fear. (Regulatory/marketing risk: `../08_roadmap/risks_and_mitigations.md`.)

## One line

We don't rent users from partners — we install **rails** that carry the exact persona we want, give the partner a premium status game and co-marketed clips in return, and convert every partner relationship into Operators who recruit for free. Close the P0 rails before launch; let the P2–P3 reach loops land on retention that already works (`../00_foundation/growth_model_and_flywheel.md`).

# Solana Distribution Playbook — Where the Attention Already Is

This is a **distribution arbitrage** doc. Solana attention is not evenly spread — it pools in a handful of surfaces (a tweet's Blink, a wallet's discover tab, a points dashboard, a trading Discord). The arbitrage is that most of these surfaces are *on-chain-native* and therefore structurally unavailable to Web2 gaming competitors: a Web2 game cannot drop a one-tap "take a seat" Action into a tweet, cannot ride a wallet's transaction-history surface, cannot earn its way onto a points aggregator. We can. Every channel below is scored on the same three things: **the arbitrage** (why attention is mispriced here), **the tactic**, **the loop it feeds** (`../00_foundation/growth_model_and_flywheel.md`), and **effort**.

The governing rule is inherited from `../01_acquisition/channel_strategy.md`: **we do not buy clicks, we light loops.** A distribution surface only counts if it deposits a durable asset — a clip, a House, a Council storyline, points gravity.

## The arbitrage thesis in one paragraph

Solana is the only chain where a financial-strategy game can be *distributed inside the transaction itself*. The wallet is the OS, the tweet is the storefront (via Blinks), and points are the gravity well. A competitor on Steam or the App Store pays per install into a walled garden; we ride rails that already carry the exact persona we want — the Sharp Trader and the Status Climber from `../00_foundation/target_segments_personas.md` — at near-zero marginal cost. That cost gap *is* the moat.

## 1. Blinks / Actions — the flagship rail

**The arbitrage.** A Blink (Dialect's Solana Actions standard) turns any tweet into a one-tap on-chain transaction. Discovery → wallet → signed action collapses into a single tap *inside the scroll*. No Web2 game can put "join my House" as a live, signable button in a tweet. This is the single most mispriced surface on Solana — near-zero cost, infinite reach, and it converts at the moment of peak intent (the moment someone sees a reveal clip).

**The tactic.**
- Every Operator's shareable reveal card ships with a **"Take a seat" Blink** — one tap routes a spectator into a practice-mode match (open, ungeofenced) or, where eligible, a real-money table.
- Every House founder gets a **"Join my House" Blink** — the K-factor rail. Embedded in their pinned tweet, their bio link, their recruiting threads.
- **Coronation Blinks** — when a Grand Operator is crowned, the ceremony tweet carries a "Challenge the throne" / "Enter the arena" Action.
- **Season-open Blinks** — a single Action that mints an Operator into the new season's ladder.

**The loop it feeds.** House referral (2) primarily — it raises *conversion-per-invite* in the growth equation. Secondarily Clip (1), because every clip becomes a recruiting surface.

**Effort.** Low-to-medium (one-time integration with Dialect; then free to scale). **Priority: maximum.**

## 2. Wallet placement & integrations — Phantom / Backpack

**The arbitrage.** The wallet is where the Solana-native persona *lives*. Phantom and Backpack have discovery surfaces — explore tabs, featured dapps, in-wallet notifications, transaction-history annotations — that point users at on-chain experiences. A spot in a wallet's "explore" rail is borrowed distribution to the exact audience, and it reads as *endorsement*, not advertising.

**The tactic.**
- **Phantom explore / featured placement** — pursue a featured slot (value exchange and integration concept in `partnerships_pipeline.md`). Lead with practice mode so the CTA is ungeofenced and friction-free.
- **Backpack-native integration** — Backpack's audience skews trader/degenerate-with-taste, the closest wallet match to our brand. Pursue deep integration (in-wallet "take a seat," xNFT-style surface if it fits the premium frame).
- **In-wallet reveal notifications** — where a partner allows it, a contested-reveal moment can ping the Operator's wallet ("Your seal is about to break").
- **Transaction-history co-brand** — a settled match shows as a legible, branded entry in wallet history, turning every Operator's own ledger into a passive ad seen by no one but reinforcing the ritual.

**The loop it feeds.** Ecosystem (7), feeding into Clip (1) and House (2) once users arrive.

**Effort.** Medium-to-high (requires partnership + integration build). **Priority: high; sequence into Phase 2.**

## 3. Quest platforms & points campaigns

**The arbitrage.** The Solana points-meta has trained an entire mercenary cohort to *do on-chain actions for rewards*. Quest platforms aggregate that cohort and rent it to protocols cheaply. The arbitrage: our points are earned by **playing and retaining** by design (`../05_economy_incentives/`), so quest traffic that would bounce off a typical airdrop farm instead converts into actual matches and W4-retained Operators.

**The tactic.**
- **Quest campaigns** — "play your first three SEALED-REVEAL hands," "found a House," "reach a contested reveal." Each quest maps to an activation milestone, not a hollow click.
- **Anti-sybil gating from day one** — quests route through proof-of-personhood and skill-weighted rating (`../05_economy_incentives/incentive_and_anti_sybil_design.md`) so we don't pay farmers for noise. (Risk register: `../08_roadmap/risks_and_mitigations.md`.)
- **Practice-mode quests are open**; real-money quests are **geofenced** to eligible regions.

**The loop it feeds.** Speculation/Points (5) + Ecosystem (7).

**Effort.** Low-to-medium per campaign. **Priority: high, but only at Phase 2 scale** — speculation lands on proven retention, never vaporware.

## 4. Points aggregators & dashboards

**The arbitrage.** Aggregators (the dashboards that track points-per-protocol across Solana) are where the mercenary cohort decides *where to deploy attention next*. Being listed with a credible "Season 0 points → token path" narrative captures intent at the exact decision point — and it's earned placement, not paid.

**The tactic.**
- **Get listed** with a clean, legible points program and a transparent (regulation-deferred, never-overclaimed) token narrative.
- **Rank for "active + retained" metrics**, not raw TVL — our edge is that our points correlate with real play, which aggregators and their users learn to trust.
- **Cross-link from aggregators into quest campaigns and Blinks** so the funnel from "I saw the points" → "I took a seat" is one hop.

**The loop it feeds.** Speculation/Points (5).

**Effort.** Low. **Priority: medium; turns on with the points program in Phase 2.**

## 5. Crypto Twitter (CT) — the gravity center

**The arbitrage.** CT is where Solana status is minted and where every other surface gets its narrative fuel. It is effectively free, and attention compounds on prior attention. CT is not a paid channel; it is the *home* of the Clip and Status loops. (Full doctrine: `../01_acquisition/crypto_twitter_playbook.md`.)

**The tactic.**
- **Reveal clips as the atomic content unit** — beautiful, status-bearing cards from the Clip Engine (`../03_virality_network_effects/spectator_and_clip_engine.md`), each carrying a "Take a seat" Blink.
- **Council movements as recurring drama** — "X entered The Council," "Y dethroned the Grand Operator." Manufactured, legitimate status drama.
- **Operator quote-tweet culture** — Operators flexing their hands *is* the distribution; we engineer the artifact to be flex-worthy.
- **Premium voice guardrails** — Royal × Institutional × Elite. Never casino, never meme-spam. "Out-play the table," never "out-predict the market."

**The loop it feeds.** Clip (1) + Status/Council (3).

**Effort.** Medium (sustained content + community ops). **Priority: maximum, always-on from Phase 0.**

## 6. Trading Discords & Telegram

**The arbitrage.** Solana's serious traders congregate in gated Discords and TG channels (alpha groups, DeFi communities, the Drift/Jupiter-adjacent crowds). These are high-trust, high-intent rooms where a *premium* offer lands and a casino offer gets banned. The arbitrage is trust transfer — an endorsement inside a sharp trading room is worth a thousand cold impressions.

**The tactic.**
- **House + Chamber homes** live here — recruiting *happens* in Discord once the loop is lit (`../04_community/`). Not top-of-funnel; conversion infrastructure.
- **Exhibition challenges seeded into alpha groups** — "best trader in [Discord] vs. our Grand Operator," streamed, clipped.
- **Chamber drafts** — Chambers recruit strong Operators directly from trading communities, turning rivalries into team rivalries.
- **Earn endorsements, don't buy posts** — convert respected voices to Operators with Houses (the KOL doctrine, `../01_acquisition/kol_and_creator_program.md`), never flat-fee shilling.

**The loop it feeds.** House (2) + Chamber (4), feeding Clip (1).

**Effort.** Medium (community ops + relationship work). **Priority: high from Phase 1.**

## 7. Co-streams & live spectacle

**The arbitrage.** A live, high-stakes Syndicate match is *televised poker for the on-chain era* — inherently watchable, inherently clippable. Co-streaming with Solana KOLs and streamers borrows their live audience and deposits a VOD plus a tail of clips that outlive the broadcast. Web2 prediction apps have no equivalent spectacle.

**The tactic.**
- **Marquee exhibition matches** co-streamed with KOL-Operators — stakes, table talk, a dramatic contested reveal as the climax.
- **Coronation broadcasts** — the Grand Operator crowning as a recurring tentpole (`../04_community/rituals_events_and_seasons.md`).
- **Clip the reveal moment** as the takeaway artifact; seed it into CT with a Blink.

**The loop it feeds.** Clip (1) + Status (3).

**Effort.** Medium-to-high (production cost ÷ the clip tail it generates). **Priority: high for tentpoles; not continuous.**

## 8. Oracle & perp partner surfaces

**The arbitrage.** Our matches resolve against a live market via an oracle (Switchboard default, Pyth secondary) and conceptually sit adjacent to the perps/DeFi venues the Sharp Trader already uses (Drift, Jupiter, marginfi, Kamino, Zeta). These partners have surfaces — docs, integration showcases, dashboards, co-marketing channels — and an audience that is *definitionally* our persona: people who already form price convictions for money. The arbitrage is audience adjacency at zero acquisition cost.

**The tactic.**
- **Oracle co-marketing** — Switchboard/Pyth showcase Syndicate as a marquee consumer of their feeds ("provably-fair settlement powered by [oracle]"); we get a credibility + distribution co-sign. (Value exchange in `partnerships_pipeline.md`.)
- **Perp-venue cross-promotion** — "you trade the move on Drift; out-play the table on Syndicate." Position as the *skill/status* complement to leveraged trading, not a competitor for the same capital.
- **Cross-quests with DeFi venues** — quests that bridge their users into a first SEALED-REVEAL hand.

**The loop it feeds.** Ecosystem (7), feeding Clip (1) and House (2).

**Effort.** Medium (partnership-gated). **Priority: high; oracle integration is launch-critical, perp co-marketing sequences into Phase 2.**

---

## 9. Memecoin-community crossovers — within premium guardrails

**The arbitrage.** Memecoin culture (Bonk and the broader Solana degen base) is the largest, most viral attention pool on the chain — and the most dangerous to our brand. The arbitrage is real but *conditional*: their reach is enormous, but a sloppy crossover reads as casino and detonates the premium positioning. Capture the reach, refuse the register.

**The tactic.**
- **Spectacle, not pandering** — invite memecoin-community figures to *exhibition matches* as challengers; let the premium production do the framing. They bring the audience; we keep the voice.
- **Co-branded tentpole events**, never co-branded chips or meme-skinned tables. The crossover is an *event*, not a product change.
- **Hard guardrails** — no meme language in our voice, no "ape in," no casino framing, ever. The crossover funnels their audience into *our* premium frame; we never adopt theirs. (Brand-dilution risk and mitigation: `../08_roadmap/risks_and_mitigations.md`.)

**The loop it feeds.** Clip (1) at the top, converting into House (2).

**Effort.** Medium (curation + brand discipline). **Priority: opportunistic, Phase 2+, only when the brand is strong enough to absorb it.**

## Sequencing (don't light it all at once)

Mirrors the flywheel's loop sequence:

- **Phase 0–1:** Blinks + CT + co-streams + trading-Discord seeding. Prove the reveal sells and seed the Clip Loop. *No points/quest spend* — speculation lands on vaporware otherwise.
- **Phase 1–2:** Wallet integrations + oracle/perp co-marketing come online; Houses recruit through Discords via Blinks.
- **Phase 2–3:** Quest platforms, points aggregators, and memecoin crossovers at full power, on proven retention.

## The arbitrage Web2 can never close

A Steam or App Store competitor pays per install into a walled garden and distributes *outside* the product. We distribute *inside the transaction*: the wallet is the storefront, the tweet is a signable button, points are gravity, and every Operator is a rail. That structural gap is permanent — it is the reason ecosystem distribution is the seventh loop and the endgame of the flywheel.
